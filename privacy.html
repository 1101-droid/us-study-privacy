INTRODUCTION

US_Study (“we”, “the app”) is an independent educational tool that helps adults prepare for the United States civics test and naturalization interview.

This Policy explains what information may be used, how it is handled, what we do not ask for, and what choices you have. By installing or using US_Study, you agree to these practices. If you do not agree, please do not use the app. US_Study is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or connected to USCIS, DHS, or any other agency of the United States government. The content is for education only. The questions come from public sources.

COMMITMENT

We ask only for what is needed to study and run the app. We prioritize on-device processing. We do not ask for sensitive immigration documents. We do not sell personal information. Credit charges, if you buy credits, are processed by Google Play.

WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT

We do not intentionally request or store: SSN, A-Number, USCIS case number, a required full legal name, a full home address, passports or other immigration documents, or a real N-400 eligibility file.

Yes/No questions in the N-400 style are practice only. We do not receive your card number. Google Play handles that.

INFORMATION WE MAY USE

4.1 Study and progress (on the phone)

The device may store: questions practiced, correct and incorrect answers, exam version (2008 — 100 questions; 2025 — 128; 65/20 — 20 questions based on the 2008 bank), language (English or Spanish), favorite answers you choose when more than one official answer is valid, approximate study time, and evaluation data (time per section, mock-interview history). That supports practice, the evaluation panel, and the weekly report. It is not sent to USCIS.

4.2 Settings

In Settings you can: change the language, update your study location (state, city, or ZIP code), and reset favorite answers. Reset deletes those favorites on the phone; it does not delete what you already sent to the developer.

4.3 Approximate location (optional)

If you use the location screen or update the area in Settings, you may enter a ZIP code, city, or state. The app may request ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION and use Android’s Geocoder to estimate state/city and show the governor, U.S. senators, and representative. This is not live tracking or commercial profiling. You may refuse location and skip ZIP. Names of national officeholders (President, Vice President, Speaker of the House, Chief Justice) may be stored on the phone and refreshed from Firebase if the cache is old. Offline, the app uses fallback names.

4.4 Voice and writing

RECORD_AUDIO is used so you can speak with the tutor Noah. Speech synthesis and recognition (SpeechRecognizer) run on the device. We do not build a permanent commercial biometric voice file. Reading, writing, and Oath practice are scored on the device. Handwriting strokes are not sent to USCIS.

4.5 Account, trial, and technical data

Device type, operating system, and language. If you sign in, the app uses your account email. The 7-day free trial belongs to that email, not to the install date. The start date is saved on the phone and on the server in Firestore (trial_emails). There is a server-side check. If you uninstall and install again, the 7 days do not restart when the cloud already has that date.

4.6 Messages, feedback, weekly report, and practice chat

Weekly report: created on the phone and shown in Inbox. It is not a message from the developer and is not uploaded to Firebase as your email. You can remove it from the view; the id stays on the phone so it is not shown again. Suggestions: the text is sent to Firebase (user_feedback). A copy may also be emailed to the operator through Formspree. A local copy is kept in Sent. Typical fields: technical user id, body, date, study location if any, language, and platform. Developer Inbox: the app may read Firebase (messages and private_messages). Deleting in the mailbox: removes the card from the screen and stores the id on the phone. It does not delete the Firebase document or the Formspree email already sent. There is no Reply button to the developer in the current version. Practice with another user has a chat between users. Those messages are stored in Firebase and deleted 24 hours after they are sent. While you are in that area, the app also stores an online-presence signal for your email (nickname, avatar, and a recent time) so others can see that you are available.

4.7 Credits

The app no longer sells weekly_sub or monthly_sub. After the 7 free days, Google Play Billing sells credits: $2 for 100 credits, $5 for 250 credits, and $10 for 500 credits. One credit is one minute. Google processes the payment. The app stores the credit balance for your email on the phone and in Firestore (subscriptions) so it can open or close the app after the trial. Outside the 7-day trial, the sections stay closed until that email has credits. Settings stays available.

ON-DEVICE PROCESSING

Whenever possible, voice, answer checking, reading, writing, and progress stay on the phone.

WRITING PRACTICE

Practice sentences are compared with the target sentence. This is educational. It does not extract an identity document.

FULL INTERVIEW PRACTICE

It may include N-400-style eligibility practice, civics, reading, writing, and the Oath. It is not an official interview and does not send data to USCIS.

NOAH

Noah may read questions, listen to answers, and guide reading, civics, and the Oath. It is not used to build a commercial biometric profile.

EVALUATION AND REVIEW

The evaluation panel and correct/incorrect results show what to practice. The weekly report summarizes study and weak areas on the phone.

STORY MODE

A United States history narrative, in English and Spanish, for study. It does not require sensitive personal data.

THIRD PARTIES

Google Firebase: account email, 7-day trial start, credit balance, incoming messages, sent suggestions, practice-with-another-user chat (deleted after 24 hours), online presence, possible officeholder-name updates, infrastructure. Formspree: email forwarding of your suggestion to the operator. Google Play: install, store listing, and credit charges. Each provider handles data under its own policy. We do not sell your information.

NOTIFICATIONS

If a version enables study reminders, you can turn them off in system or app settings. If that feature is not on in your install, none are sent.

RETENTION

Study data, favorites, the weekly report, local Sent items, and deleted ids stay on the phone while the app is installed. Uninstalling removes local data. Data already written to Firebase or sent through Formspree may remain there as needed for support and operation. To request server-side deletion: lujanjesus81@gmail.com. Do not send immigration documents in that email.

SECURITY

We take reasonable steps; no system is 100% secure. Protect the phone (screen lock, updates, no unsupervised shared use).

CHILDREN

The app is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that happened, contact us.

YOUR CHOICES

  • Do not enter a ZIP or grant location.
  • Change language and location in Settings.
  • Reset favorites in Settings.
  • Delete mailbox cards (on the phone only).
  • Do not send suggestions.
  • Do not buy credits.
  • Uninstall the app.

Pedimos solo lo necesario para estudiar y operar la app. Priorizamos el procesamiento en el teléfono. No pedimos documentos de inmigración sensibles. No vendemos información personal. Los cobros de créditos, si usted compra créditos, los procesa Google Play.

LO QUE NO RECOPILAMOS

No solicitamos ni guardamos de forma intencional: SSN, A-Number, número de caso USCIS, nombre legal completo obligatorio, dirección residencial completa, pasaportes u otros documentos de inmigración, ni un expediente real de elegibilidad N-400.

INFORMACIÓN QUE PODEMOS UTILIZAR

4.1 Estudio y progreso (en el teléfono)

Pueden guardarse en el dispositivo: preguntas practicadas, aciertos y fallos, versión de examen (2008 — 100 preguntas; 2025 — 128; 65/20 — 20 preguntas basadas en el banco 2008), idioma (inglés o español), respuestas favoritas que usted elija cuando hay más de una respuesta oficial válida, tiempo de estudio aproximado y datos del panel de evaluación (tiempo por sección, historial de simulacros). Eso alimenta la práctica, la evaluación y el reporte semanal. No se envía a USCIS.

4.2 Ajustes

En Ajustes puede: cambiar el idioma, actualizar su ubicación de estudio (estado, ciudad o código postal) y restablecer las respuestas favoritas. El reset borra esas favoritas en el teléfono; no borra lo que ya se haya enviado al desarrollador.

4.3 Ubicación aproximada (opcional)

Si usa la pantalla de ubicación o actualiza la zona en Ajustes, puede escribir código postal, ciudad o estado. La app puede pedir ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION y ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION y usar el Geocoder de Android para estimar estado/ciudad y mostrar gobernador, senadores y representante. No es rastreo continuo ni perfil comercial. Puede no dar ubicación ni ZIP. Los nombres de cargos nacionales (presidente, vicepresidente, presidente de la Cámara, presidente del Tribunal Supremo) pueden guardarse en el teléfono y actualizarse desde Firebase si el caché está viejo. Sin red se usan valores de reserva.

4.4 Voz y escritura

Permiso RECORD_AUDIO para hablar con el tutor Noah. La síntesis de voz y el reconocimiento (SpeechRecognizer) se usan en el dispositivo. No construimos un archivo biométrico permanente de su voz. La práctica de lectura, escritura y juramento se evalúa en el aparato. Los trazos de escritura no se envían a USCIS.

4.5 Cuenta, prueba y datos técnicos

Tipo de dispositivo, sistema e idioma. Si inicia sesión, la app usa el correo de su cuenta. La prueba gratuita de 7 días es de ese correo, no de la fecha de instalación. La fecha de inicio se guarda en el teléfono y en el servidor, en Firestore (trial_emails). Sí hay una comprobación en el servidor. Si desinstala y vuelve a instalar, los 7 días no se reinician cuando la nube ya tiene esa fecha.

4.6 Mensajes, sugerencias, reporte semanal y chat de práctica

Reporte semanal: se genera en el teléfono y se muestra en Recibidos. No es un mensaje del desarrollador y no se sube a Firebase como su correo. Puede borrarlo de la vista; el id queda en el teléfono para no mostrarlo otra vez. Sugerencias: el texto se envía a Firebase (user_feedback). Puede enviarse también una copia por correo mediante Formspree al operador. En Enviados queda una copia local. Datos típicos del envío: identificador técnico de usuario, texto, fecha, ubicación de estudio si existe, idioma y plataforma. Recibidos del desarrollador: la app puede leer Firebase (messages y private_messages). Borrar en el buzón: quita el mensaje de la pantalla y guarda el id en el teléfono. No borra el documento ya guardado en Firebase ni el correo ya enviado por Formspree. No hay botón de responder al desarrollador en la versión actual. En Practicar con otro usuario hay un chat entre usuarios. Esos mensajes se guardan en Firebase y se borran a las 24 horas de enviarse. Mientras está en esa zona, la app también guarda una señal de presencia en línea de su correo (apodo, avatar y una hora reciente) para que otros vean que está disponible.

4.7 Créditos

La app ya no vende weekly_sub ni monthly_sub. Después de los 7 días gratis, Google Play Billing vende créditos: $2 por 100 créditos, $5 por 250 créditos y $10 por 500 créditos. Un crédito es un minuto. Google procesa el pago. La app guarda el saldo de créditos de su correo en el teléfono y en Firestore (subscriptions) para abrir o cerrar la app después de la prueba. Fuera de los 7 días, las secciones siguen cerradas hasta que ese correo tenga créditos. Ajustes sigue disponible.
