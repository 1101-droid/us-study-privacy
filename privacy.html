1. Introduction

US_Study128 (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is an independent educational application developed by Jesus L. Goebel. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our App. By using US_Study128, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

a) Information you provide directly

  • ZIP code or city (used only to show local representatives, senators, and governor)
  • Voice recordings and speech data (when you speak answers to Noah)
  • Practice progress and review history (questions you mark as difficult)

b) Information collected automatically

  • Device information (device model, operating system version)
  • App usage data (features used, session duration, practice results)
  • Approximate location (only if you grant permission, used solely for local civics questions)

c) Payment information

Subscription payments are processed by Google Play. We do not store your credit card or payment details.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and improve the educational features of the App (including personalized local civics questions)
  • To enable the voice recognition and AI tutoring features of Noah
  • To save your progress and difficult questions in the Smart Review system
  • To process subscriptions and manage your access
  • To analyze App performance and fix technical issues
  • To respond to your support requests

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Voice and Speech Data

When you use the voice features (speaking answers to Noah), your voice is processed in real time for speech recognition. We do not permanently store your voice recordings unless necessary for technical improvement, and in that case the data is anonymized.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Billing – for subscription management
  • Google Civic Information API – to obtain official local representative data based on ZIP code
  • Speech recognition services (to enable Noah’s voice features)

These services have their own privacy policies. We only share the minimum data necessary for the App to function.

6. Data Retention

  • Practice progress and review data are stored until you delete your data or uninstall the App.
  • Voice data is processed temporarily and not retained longer than necessary.
  • You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

US_Study128 is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Request a copy of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at: lujanjesus81@gmail.com

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new version in the App or on this page and updating the “Last updated” date.
